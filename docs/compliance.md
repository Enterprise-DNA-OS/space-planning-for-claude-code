# Workplace evidence and planning checks

Checked 22 October 2026. These checks support a human workplace review. They do not certify a building, establish lawful occupancy or replace a competent person's assessment.

| Rule | What runs | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| EMERGENCY-PLAN | A building used by a scenario needs an emergency-plan reference and an internal review date that has not passed. | NZ General Risk and Workplace Management Regulations 2016, regulation 14, requires an emergency plan and its maintenance. WorkSafe says to review plans when the workplace changes. The date and reference test is an internal evidence prompt, not the statutory test. |
| CAPACITY-EVIDENCE | Every used floor needs a reference supporting its recorded capacity. | WorkSafe workplace-facilities guidance covers safe movement and work space. Our numeric planning capacity is supplied by the operator, not calculated from law. |
| CAPACITY | Assigned seats must not exceed recorded floor capacity. | Internal planning rule. No statutory seats-per-area formula is implemented. |
| PEAK-COVERAGE | Seats per department allocation must cover the rounded-up peak assumption for its assigned people. | Internal planning rule. A hybrid-work assumption is not observed occupancy. |
| HEADCOUNT | The sum of assigned people across a scenario must equal each department's headcount. | Internal completeness rule. |
| MOVE-TASK | Every blocking move task must have completion evidence. | Internal change-control rule, supporting the human workplace review. |

Sources:

- [New Zealand legislation: regulation 14](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/regulation/public/2016/0013/latest/DLM6727360.html).
- [WorkSafe: workplace emergency plans](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/managing-health-and-safety/businesses/general-requirements-for-workplaces/emergency-plans/).
- [WorkSafe: workplace and facilities requirements](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/managing-health-and-safety/businesses/general-requirements-for-workplaces/workplace-and-facilities-requirements/).
- [Safe Work Australia: emergency plans overview](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/managing-health-and-safety/emergency-plans-and-procedures/overview). This is model-law guidance. An Australian implementation must identify the applicable state or territory rules before operational use.

No fixed legal annual review interval is assumed. The operator sets a review date. A move requires the actual plan and facilities to be reviewed, not just a date entered here. Record the human decision and its evidence. Actor labels do not authenticate a qualified reviewer.

An approval snapshot covers building, floor, department, allocation and task inputs. Any later change requires approval again before activation. Local history records decisions, but database owners can alter the data and history. Production deployments need access and backup controls.
